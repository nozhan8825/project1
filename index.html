History BMW

The history of BMW begins with the company Rapp Motorenwerke. In April 1917, when Karl Rapp left the company, the name was changed to Bayerische Motorenwerke. The factory's first product was the BMW 3A aircraft engine. This engine was known for its low fuel consumption and good performance at high altitudes. As a result of the many orders for the BMW 3A engine from the German army, the company expanded rapidly.

company BMW

The company was ranked 46th on the Forbes Global 2000 list in 2023.[9] The company also has a distinguished history in motorsports, particularly in touring car racing, sports car racing, and the Isle of Man TT. BMW initially focused on producing aircraft engines during World War I, a legacy that influenced the company's engineering emphasis on performance and precision. After post-war restrictions on aircraft production, BMW turned to motorcycle production in the 1920s and later entered the automobile market, establishing itself as a major player in the automotive industry.
